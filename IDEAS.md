# Jarvis: 50 feature ideas (proposed 2026-10-07, not built)

Owner picks by number ("build 11 and 29"). When one is built, move it to FUNCTIONS.md and mark it here.
Items marked (W#/A#) already sit as 💡 in FUNCTIONS.md; this list promotes them.

## A. Daily rhythm
1. Morning briefing: one spoken rundown at wake (weather, today's jobs, unpaid invoices, overnight portfolio move, anything needing a decision).
2. Food on your route: on a drive, suggests open stops on the path (Wawa, Chick-fil-A), learns what he actually picks.
3. Night-before plan: tomorrow's jobs, weather, bring list, alarm, once, at a set hour.
4. Smart alarm: sets tomorrow's alarm from the first job plus drive time; asks once.
5. Friday review: money in, jobs done, stock P/L, what slipped.
6. Sunday week-ahead: booked jobs, gaps, bills due.
7. Sleep nudge: "It's 1 am and you have a 9 am install", one-tap alarm.
8. Weather heads-ups (W9): rain on a tint day, ice for the R8, heat for film curing.

## B. Checking on you
9. Promise tracker: "You told Mike you'd call back by 3."
10. Follow-through nudges: "You said you'd order the amp. Still want it?" after 24 h.
11. Decision queue: "What's waiting?" lists everything pending on him.
12. Escalation ladder: important and unanswered goes push, then phone call, then siren (wake-up call).
13. ETA share: texts a chosen person automatically when his ETA slips.
14. Safety check-in: stopped long somewhere odd, or hard stop plus OBD drop, then "You alright, sir?"
15. Project callbacks: "How did the Optima wall come out?" from long-term memory.
16. Voice-notes inbox: "note to self" saved, tagged, weekly digest.

## C. Shop and money
17. Job log by voice (W4).
18. Quote by voice (W6), uses labour rate and parts prices.
19. Invoice chaser: draft reminders at day 3, 7, 14 for approval.
20. Deposit watch: booked jobs with no deposit get flagged.
21. Low-stock reorder: "Last roll of 5% film" goes on the list with a price check.
22. Close-out checklist (W8): lights, compressor, bay door, cash, lock, unfinished jobs.
23. Review requests: after follow-up, sends the Google review link (approved).
24. Slow-day filler: calendar gap triggers a text/post idea for past customers.
25. Before/after photo prompts at job start/end, filed by customer and vehicle.
26. History lookup: "Did we do that Tahoe?" from the job log.
27. Deadlines radar: rent, insurance, taxes, registration, licences at 30/7/1 days.
28. Tax set-aside nudge on deposits.

## D. Car and travel
29. Maintenance schedule from OBD mileage and dates (oil, tires, inspection, registration).
30. Fuel stop on route when the tank is low.
31. Where I parked: saved at engine off.
32. Leave-now alerts with live traffic (A12).
33. Pre-departure closure/crash warning on the usual route.
34. Mileage log: business vs personal for taxes.

## E. Home and shop
35. Arrive-home scene: lights, music, speaker by time of day.
36. Away check: "Lights, garage, door?" when leaving.
37. Bay door open after close: alert after 20 min.
38. Tool tracking with BLE tags (parked).
39. After-hours camera snapshot on motion.
40. Compressor/heater/lights left on at close: alert plus off-on-confirm.

## F. Markets
41. Pre-market brief and closing recap of positions.
42. Earnings/news heads-up the day before for held and watched tickers.
43. Trade journal by voice: why he bought; weekly outcome review.
44. FOMO/concentration guard: warns on chasing a big green day or over-weighting one ticker.
45. Monthly P/L statement, stocks and crypto, for taxes.

## G. Life admin and business
46. Package tracking from email: "Your amp is at the door."
47. Bill-due radar from email/texts.
48. Email triage (A15): top 3 daily plus drafted replies.
49. Upwork job hunter for Nexus: matching posts alerted, proposal drafted.
50. MyGuru daily numbers plus YouTube cadence nudge.
